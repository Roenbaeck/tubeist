Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tubeist processes your video on your iPhone and sends streams directly to YouTube when you choose to go live.

Tubeist is published by Up to Change AB and developed by Lars Rönnbäck. We do not operate a Tubeist analytics, advertising, streaming or account server. The app does not send your recordings, credentials or usage analytics to us. Website visits and information you choose to send to support are described separately below.

Camera, microphone and local processing

Tubeist uses the camera and microphone with your permission to record or stream video and audio. Camera controls, encoding, styles, effects and overlay composition run on your device. Tubeist does not request access to your location or contacts.

Recordings and optional highlight clips are saved in the app’s local Documents folder. Upload buffering can temporarily store waiting media on the device; those buffers are cleaned up automatically and are not a saved recording. Local recording works without signing in to Google.

YouTube and Google authorization

When you start a stream, Tubeist sends video, audio and the technical requests needed for delivery directly to YouTube’s ingestion service. YouTube handles that content under your channel and broadcast settings. We do not receive a copy of the stream.

Tubeist uses YouTube API Services. Optional Google authorization lets it access your channel identity, find or create an HLS stream, read and apply broadcast settings, and check live status and ingest health. It can also read available viewer counts for optional status displays. These functions act on the channel you authorize; they do not create a separate Tubeist account.

Your stream key and Google access and refresh tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. App preferences, including your saved broadcast settings, are stored locally. Google handles sign-in; Tubeist does not ask you to provide your Google password to Up to Change.

Google and YouTube receive the requests made to their services and process information under the Google Privacy Policy. Broadcast visibility and retention are controlled through YouTube. A private broadcast is still sent to and stored by YouTube; it is not an offline recording.

Web overlays and external status

If you supply a web overlay or external status URL, Tubeist requests content from that provider. The provider can receive ordinary connection data such as your IP address and request details. Web pages may use cookies or local storage and may include third-party content. Their privacy practices depend on the service you choose. No overlay provider is contacted merely by visiting this website.

Apple services and Watch features

Apple handles App Store distribution and in-app purchase transactions. Tubeist checks purchase entitlements to unlock the features you bought; we do not receive your payment card details. Optional Live Activities and Watch features share session information through Apple’s device frameworks. Optional alerts use system notification permissions.

Apple services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Local app data may be included in device backups you enable.

Local diagnostics

The in-app Journal keeps diagnostic entries to help you understand errors and connection issues. It is not uploaded automatically to us. Entries can include device and app information, request results and channel details. Stream keys and authorization tokens are omitted or redacted by the app. If you copy or share a log, review its contents first.

Removing data and access

  • Delete local recordings and highlights through Files. Copies you exported or shared must be removed from their destinations separately.
  • Use Settings → Privacy and Credentials → Remove YouTube Credentials to remove the saved stream key and Google authorization from Tubeist.
  • Revoke Tubeist’s authorization through your Google Account connections. Manage or delete broadcasts in YouTube Studio.
  • Deleting the app removes its local files and preferences. Keychain items and device backups follow Apple’s platform behavior; use the credential-removal control before uninstalling if you want to remove those credentials explicitly.

Disconnecting Tubeist does not delete content already sent to YouTube. There is no developer-operated Tubeist account to delete, and we cannot directly delete information in your Google or Apple accounts.

This website

This website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies, external fonts or embedded third-party media. Images and styles are served locally. Links to YouTube, the App Store, GitHub or Discord take you to those services when you select them.

The web server may process ordinary connection information, including your IP address, requested page, browser information and request time, to deliver and secure the site. Any hosting access logs are separate from app data. Contact us about website logging or a privacy request.

Support correspondence

If you email support@uptochange.com, your email address, message and attachments are processed to respond to your request. Send only the information needed to investigate the issue. You can use the same address to ask about or request deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations. GitHub and Discord handle posts made on their services under their own policies.

Children

Tubeist is not directed to children under 13. It has no developer-operated advertising or tracking SDKs. Google and Apple set the account and age requirements for their services.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its date when the app or these practices change. For privacy questions, email support@uptochange.com.
