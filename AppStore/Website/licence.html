Use it for your work

You may use Tubeist for personal projects, paid productions, business activities, education and monetized broadcasts. Up to Change imposes no restriction on commercial use, and requires no separate commercial licence, permission or royalty.

App Store distribution

The licence presented with your App Store download governs distribution and use of that copy of the app. Apple provides a Standard End User Licence Agreement where no custom agreement is supplied. Tubeist’s permission for commercial production does not add a fee or subscription.

Optional purchase

The free app includes camera controls, recording, YouTube streaming and web overlays. Styles and Effects is an optional one-time in-app purchase for the visual styles and effects. The current regional price is shown by the App Store. Apple handles the transaction and refund process. Buying the effects is not a requirement for commercial use.
